# ABOUT US
OUR STORY
RAYANCO Scraps is one of the most reputed trader, supplier, processing & recycling of scrap for number of companies in the steel industry in Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. It is by far the largest trader of metal scrap brought into the country, having processing approximately 500,000 metric tons of scrap per year. Being reputation conscious, we trade with the most reputed suppliers, offering only superior quality scrap. We collect many types of metal waste from our valuable clients and customers; then store them in our vast yard. We accurately weight the metal waste and carefully segregate and categorize them using our state-of-the art-equipment. Our company is located in Eastern province of Kingdom , with branches in Al Jubail, Riyadh, Dammam, Abqaiq, Jeddah. We are considered one of the foremost companies in this field, that we are competitive with international markets (in LME), which has matched the growth and prosperity of our Kingdom.
# VISION
OUR VISION
As it was from the very beginning, the vision of our company is to provide quality of shredded material and other scrap metal products to meet the needs of our customer’s quality standards. We aim to be the top of mind metal processing and recycling company in the Gulf Region.
